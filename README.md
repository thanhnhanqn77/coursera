# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple command-line Bash script designed to calculate simple interest based on the principal amount, annual rate of interest, and time period provided by the user.

## Formula
$$\text{Simple Interest} = \frac{P \times T \times R}{100}$$

Where:
- **P** = Principal amount
- **T** = Time period (in years)
- **R** = Annual interest rate (%)

## Input Fields
- `Principal`: The initial sum of money invested or borrowed.
- `Rate of interest`: The annual percentage interest rate.
- `Time period`: Duration of investment/loan in years.

## Output
- `Simple Interest`: The total interest earned or accrued.
